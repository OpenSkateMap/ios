# LibraryMatchKit

**Purpose:** Finds photos in the person's library taken at spots on the map, on the device (PhotoKit and Vision only), for Contribute's "From Your Photos". Nothing is uploaded here: a photo leaves the phone only when the person adds it to a spot through the normal Suggest an Edit.
**Tier:** Services
**Project deps:** `SQLiteKit`, `SpotModelKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `SQLiteData`; tests: `CustomDump`)
**Public Factory keys:** `photoLibrary`, `imageClassifier` (`.singleton`, `.onPreview`), `libraryMatchEngine` (`.singleton`), `libraryMatchSession` (`@MainActor`, `.singleton`: its phase follows the device's photo access, not the account, and it keeps no account data; the account-end handler's `LibraryMatchEngine.reset()` cancels its scan and clears the tables, and a scan cancelled that way leaves the phase `.unknown` for the next refresh. Account-scoping it would need APIClientKit, the most volatile module, for the scope alone.)
**Entry points:**
- `LibraryMatchSession` — `@MainActor @Observable` facade. `phase` (`Phase`, one enum): `.unknown` until access is read, `.notAsked`, `.denied`, `.scanning(progress:)`, `.ready`, `.limited` (ready, having looked only at the photos the person shared). `refresh()` reads access off the main actor without asking and, once it's granted, scans what changed (the app's foreground and Contribute appearing call it; a scan under way with the same access is joined, one under other access replaced). `findMyShots()` is the only place the system's prompt is shown (full access, `.readWrite`). `skip(_:)` (Not This One) and `markUsed(assetIDs:)` (the photos went out with an edit).
- `LibraryMatchEngine` — actor, the only writer of `library_match` and `library_scan` (SpotModelKit's `DatabaseSchema.libraryMatches`). `scan(isLimited:onProgress:)` runs off the main actor (`@concurrent`): the public, not demolished spots from `spot_index`; the library's photos with a location, all of them or only what changed since the stored `PHPersistentChangeToken` (all again when the token expired, when access went between limited and full, or when the spots changed: `LibraryMatcher.digest` of their ids and places); each photo within 50 m of a spot is classified on a 360 px local thumbnail (never downloaded from iCloud) and scored; the best per spot and day is written. A complete read replaces every waiting match; a partial one those of the photos it read again or lost. Skipped and used rows stay and keep their spot and day from coming back. A new scan replaces the one under way (its caller gets `.cancelled`); `reset()` cancels and clears (a scan that began before it writes nothing: `generation`).
- `LibraryMatcher` — pure: `Locator` (spots sorted by latitude, the nearest within 50 m), `score` (Vision's labels: `skateboarding` and `skateboard` first, `skatepark` only at a park spot, street architecture such as `stairs`, `street`, `sidewalk`, `bench` next, then newer and closer; nil for a clear non-match: food, rooms, documents, or a selfie (a face over 8% of the photo; a skater in a full shot is under 1%), unless a skate or street label is there too; `people` alone isn't one, since a skater mid-air with the board out of frame gets only `people` and `adult`; nothing seen is kept, so a photo without a local thumbnail, or one the simulator can't classify, still shows), `best(of:)` (one per spot and calendar day, the best score, then newer, then closer), `day(of:in:)` (the device calendar's day, `yyyy-MM-dd`). The weights are first guesses, to tune against real libraries.
- `PhotoLibrary` — manager: `access()`, `requestAccess()`, `changes(since:)` (images in the person's own library with a location, no screenshots or shared albums; `LibraryChanges`), `image(_:_:_:)` (a thumbnail, iCloud allowed only when asked: the cards), `imageData(_:)` (the original's bytes for the photo intake, iCloud allowed). Live is PhotoKit; `.preview` answers "not asked".
- `ImageClassifier` — manager: `ImageAnalysis`, Vision's `ClassifyImageRequest` labels with their confidence and the share of the photo the largest face covers (`DetectFaceRectanglesRequest`). The simulator can't run the classifier ("Failed to create espresso context"), so there every match rests on place and date.

**Ownership map:**
- Matches and where the last scan stopped → SQLite (`LibraryMatch`, `LibraryScan`), written by `LibraryMatchEngine` only; screens read them (ContributeKit's `LibraryMatchesRequest`).
- Access, and whether a scan runs and how far it got → `LibraryMatchSession`.
- The scan under way → `LibraryMatchEngine.running`, replaced by a newer one, cancelled by `reset()`.

## Rules

- Never ask for photo access at launch or from anywhere but Find My Shots (HIG). The purpose string (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`) is in the app's Info.plist and `InfoPlist.strings`, and says photos stay on the phone.
- Nothing read from the library is logged, stored beyond the match rows, or sent; the privacy manifest needs no change for it.
- Tests use `FakePhotoLibrary` (a library in memory whose classifier answers by hand; a held read lets a test look at a scan under way, and stops when the scan is cancelled), `AppDatabase.inMemory`, a fixed `now` and London's calendar.
