# SpotsMapKit

**Purpose:** The Discover tab: every curated spot as a photo pin on a full-screen muted map that zooms out into the satellite globe, with clusters, the locate button, and Discover in a panel the tab owns under the system tab bar (search, the city's spots best rated first, filters the pins follow too), and the spot sheet over everything.
**Tier:** Feature
**Project deps:** `SpotsKit`, `SpotModelKit`, `ImageKit`, `LocationKit`, `NotificationsKit` (the map's city for New Spots Near You), `AnalyticsKit`, `ComponentKit`, `SQLiteKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `PinLayout`, `SQLiteData`, `SwiftMacros`; tests: `CustomDump`, `SnapshotTesting`)
**Public Factory keys:** `addSpotScreen` (promised; the app binds it to ContributeKit's `NewSpotSheetController`), `newCollectionScreen` (promised; CollectionDetailKit's `CollectionScreens.newCollection()`)
**Entry points:**
- `SpotsMapViewController(spotSheet:)` — the screen; the app passes `SpotSheet` (how to make the sheet and its lowest detent height), since features never import each other. `openSpot(id:)` is how a shared link opens a spot: search closes, `SpotsMapModel.open(spotID:)` looks it up (syncing first when the index doesn't have it yet), flies there and selects it; a spot that isn't there tells the person (`MapNotice`).
- `DiscoverSheetController` — Discover, a map card (Ertem, 2026-10-14), in a panel the tab owns under the system tab bar (Ertem, 2026-10-11):
  - Collapsed: the search field and the +, a menu (Ertem, 2026-10-20: spot first, so the order is fixed): New Spot (the app's `addSpotScreen`), then New Collection (`newCollectionScreen`: the name prompt, then the collection's page). Risen: the place's name and its count on one line ("London" in title2 bold, "· 2 spots" in body; no city menu, Ertem 2026-10-14: the map picks the city), the filter bar (the Filters sheet, then Kind, Condition and Obstacles menus that stay open for several picks, filled while they narrow anything), and every spot of the city best rated first (kind and first obstacles). The Spot Score orders the list but is never shown (Ertem, 2026-10-14). The list is where the map rests (`DiscoverPlace`; Ertem 2026-10-21: nothing remembered from an earlier place): one of our cities when the map's center is in it, otherwise the spots in view under the place's name (MapKit's city, else its region, else "This Area"). A place with no spots says so after its name ("Badalona · No spots here yet"); zoomed out past city scale there's no place (Find a City). Only a city tells notifications.
  - Dynamic Type: the field's text stops at 34 pt and its placeholder is just "Search" at accessibility sizes (a longer one would shrink to fit); section headers stop at 32 pt and See All at 28 pt, so "Your Collections" doesn't break mid-word. At accessibility sizes spot and place rows wrap, and collection cards widen with their names (up to 264 pt) so a name like "Favorites" stays one word. Raised to large in a narrow window, the panel hides the map and locate from VoiceOver (`accessibilityViewIsModal`), until the spot sheet pushes it down. A narrowing Filters chip also changes its glyph with Differentiate Without Color. Collection cards and rows answer Voice Control by their visible names.
  - Search lives in it: the field's focus raises the panel to large, the + turns into ×, and the list shows Recently Viewed (with a text Clear in its header, like See All: a second × under the field's read badly, Ertem 2026-10-19) and Around Here, then spots by name and MapKit's places. A pick closes search; a spot opens its sheet, a place flies the map there and gets the system's marker with its name (`SpotsMapModel.placeMark`) until the next search or spot. A place is looked up first (`SpotSearchModel.resolve(_:)`): its row shows a spinner until MapKit answers, and search stays open with a failure toast if it can't be found.
  - Empty states say what to do: no city yet (Search), or no spot passes the filters (Clear Filters). They sit below Your Collections, never over the cards.
  - Show on Map (a collection card's menu, or `showCollection(_:)` from a collection page) frames the collection and lowers a full-height panel to medium (`revealMap()`), so the map shows; tapping a card only lists it and keeps the detent. A listed collection's chip (its name and ✕, which shows every spot again) leads the filter bar, followed by an ⓘ chip that opens the collection's page (Ertem, 2026-10-19). The listed collection is a copy (`CollectionFocus`) the model keeps in step with the stored collections: one of the account's own that goes (deleted here or on another device, or left) lets go of the map and the list, one that changes shows its new name and spots (Ertem, 2026-10-20: a deleted collection stayed selected); one opened from a link was never stored and stays.
  - Why not a UIKit sheet: a presented sheet covers the tab bar, and a sheet confined to one tab under its bar "isn't currently supported" (Apple, developer forums thread 708728). The two earlier tries (a standalone bar inside the sheet crossfading with the system one; the tab bar controller inside the sheet) both made the bar jump or change. Now there's one bar, the system's, and switching tabs is the system's switch.
  - A child view controller of the map: a glass panel inset 8 pt, fixed corners on top and the screen's curve at the bottom. Collapsed shows the header ("Discover", the count, a glass + for New Spot through the app's `addSpotScreen`) right above the tab bar; medium is half the screen; large is near the top, opaque and edge to edge (it fades opaque from medium to large, as an iOS 26 sheet does). The list fades in as it rises off collapsed. It opens at medium (Ertem, 2026-10-30) and never closes.
  - In a wide window (`UITraitCollection.placesCardBesideMap`: regular width or compact height: landscape, the iPhone Duo's inner display) it's a card on the leading edge (Ertem, 2026-10-19): 45% of the room between its side margins (320–400 pt); the same three detents, glass at every one. Its margins are the system map card's (`besideMapEdges`, measured on iOS 27): 23 pt from the screen's edges, the safe area on the sensor housing's side (`UIWindow.deviceTopEdge`; in landscape iOS reports the housing's inset on both sides). It reaches the bottom margin (the logo moved to the trailing corner, Ertem 2026-10-30). Below collapsed it keeps that height and slides away (the spot sheet covering it). The list keeps the card's tallest height while a drag resizes the card, so it isn't laid out again. Beside the map nothing in the card insets by the safe area (header, `CardListCell` rows and headers): the card's place already does. Across the bottom they keep clear of side safe areas (the Duo's outer display puts the tab bar on the trailing edge). The collection cards' and the chips' scrollers never adjust for it (they lay out their own margins; an adjusted inset doubled them). `CardListCell` lays itself out again when its size classes change: a rotation lays cells out before they change, and some rows kept the mirrored inset (Ertem, 2026-10-19). The list runs up under the chips (in search, the field): nothing shows under the search field and the title, and rows fade out across the chips band (a gradient mask on the list's container, with the soft edge effect), no hard edge at its top (Ertem, 2026-10-19). The city's spots have a "Spots" header like "Your Collections". Turning from one landscape side to the other lays the map out again (`viewWillTransition`), since size and safe area stay the same.
  - The header drags it (`headerPanned`); the list's own drag moves it first and scrolls once it's at large, handing back at the list's top (`ScrollRegulator` on `RegulatedCollectionView`). Releases settle on the projected detent with UIKit's sheet spring; the grabber's tap cycles detents (VoiceOver: "Sheet Grabber").
  - `onMove` gives the map the panel's edge: `nil` for a drag frame, or the animator it springs with, which the chrome joins.
  - The spot sheet pushes it off as one sheet hides the one under it (UIKit's `_setHiddenAncestorSheetID:` only works between UIKit sheets, and this panel isn't one; Ertem, 2026-10-12): `setCovered(_:)` takes how far, which the map derives from the spot sheet's edge (`coverDiscover()`): gone while the sheet is at or above its lowest detent, and in step with it below that, so the panel sinks as the sheet rises, rises with a swipe down on it (showing above it once the swipe is a little over a third of the way, as the system's hidden sheet does), and springs with it when it's let go.
- `RegulatedCollectionView` + `ScrollRegulator` — a collection view whose own pan proposes an offset and a regulator keeps or rewrites it, and decides whether a release coasts. Private API installed on this subclass only (`handlePan:`, `_scrollViewWillEndDraggingWithDeceleration:`), with the type encodings checked; on a mismatch the list just scrolls and the panel moves from its header.
- `SpotsMapModel` — `visibleRegion` (at rest: saving, queries), the `filter` (`SpotFilter`, one per run) both queries go through, the `place` the map rests on and its `citySpots` (`SpotIndex.inCity`, or `SpotIndex.inArea` for the box in view outside our cities; ranked by score; area names cached per tenth of a degree, since MapKit rate-limits reverse geocoding), viewport `@FetchAll` reloaded 150 ms after the map rests (box 1.25× the region, filtered); pins re-applied at most every 150 ms; camera requests; locate (only the locate button asks; once allowed, every launch opens on the person with the dot: the last fix the system has at once, then a fresh one moves the map there unless the person or a link already did, Ertem 2026-10-19); the shared `SpotSelection`. Adopting a city also tells notifications (New Spots Near You).
- `SpotAnnotation`, `SpotAnnotationView`, `SpotClusterAnnotationView`, `PinCardView`, `PinArt`.
- **Analytics** (AnalyticsKit): `SpotsMapModel.select(_:from:)` and `focus(on:from:)` take where a spot was opened from (map, list, recent, nearby, search, link) and send `spot_opened`; `focus(onPlace:)` sends `place_opened`; `setFilter(_:)` sends `filters_changed`. The app's own selection changes (closing, restoring) pass no source and send nothing.
- `SpotSearchModel` — search's state: Recently Viewed (`RecentSpots`, per device) and Around Here before typing; spots by name from SQLite and places from MapKit (`PlaceCompleter`) after.
- `FiltersSheet` — Filters as our option sheet (ComponentKit's `OptionSheetViewController`, Ertem 2026-10-14: like our modals): Kind, Condition and Obstacles as `ToggleChipsView` tags and Clear All; it edits a copy, and its button counts the city's spots the copy would leave ("Show 2 Spots") and applies it.
- `MapGlobeProjection`, `GlobeFlight` (`FlightPath`, `GlobeCameraMath`) — the globe and the flights: a first launch opens on the planet and flies down into the busiest city; a far pick arcs up and down; near moves stay MapKit's animation.
- `SheetEdgeTracker` — a sheet's top edge, in step with how UIKit moves it (no display link). A drag reports the sheet's frame from its pan recognizers' callbacks, where the new frame is set but not yet drawn, so the chrome moves in the same frame. Letting go reports the edge with the very `CASpringAnimation` UIKit settles the sheet with (mass 1, stiffness 333.3, damping 36.5 on iOS 27), and the chrome animates with the same `UISpringTimingParameters`. Presentation moves it to where the sheet will rest, with the sheet's own presentation spring: during the transition the sheet already has its final size but not its floating inset (iOS 26 leaves as much room below as at the sides), so its frame alone would overshoot by that inset. A dismissal not made by a swipe moves it alongside the transition coordinator. A swipe that dismisses the sheet is followed like a drag: the transition's alongside animations don't follow the finger (they run at once, in 0.5 s), so its pan reports every frame, and on release the final edge (off screen, or back at the detent if given up) with the spring UIKit ends a sheet's drag with, which moves it frame by frame with no `CAAnimation` to read: `-[_UISheetInteraction draggingEndedInSource:]`, response 0.344 s, damping ratio 1, or 0.8 at 1000 pt/s or faster (`SheetSpeedThreshold`), no initial velocity; fitted to the sheet's frames within 0.4 pt. The map tracks the spot sheet with it; Discover's panel reports its own edge through `onMove`, and the Apple logo, Legal and locate ride the higher of the two.

- **Spots at one place** (Ertem 2026-10-11): MapKit clusters by coordinate, so a cluster whose spots are within 30 m of each other never comes apart by zooming. Its tap lists them instead ("2 Spots Here", a native menu from a button over the stack, `SpotClusterAnnotationView.isInseparable`), and a pick selects that spot. Other clusters zoom in. Not spreading the pins: moving them put a spot on the river (tried and reverted).
- **Private spots** (from the account's collections) draw like any pin, with a small lock badge on the card's corner (`SpotAnnotationView`, a pre-drawn image, no live layer effects); VoiceOver says "private". Pins and clusters answer Voice Control by the title shown under them; a selected pin grows without its pop with Reduce Motion.

**Ownership map:**
- Pins on screen → reconciled by id and version from `SpotsMapModel.spots` and the box they were queried for. A pin leaves when its own area was queried and it wasn't returned (removed on the server), when the filter leaves it out, or when more than `annotationCap` are on the map; the selected pin always stays.
- The open spot → `SpotSelection` (map sets it on tap; the sheet clears it as a swipe-down starts, and a swipe given up restores it, so the pin deselects alongside the sheet). The map lets go of its sheet once that dismissal completes.
- Recently viewed spots → `UserDefaults` (`map.recentSpots`, at most 20), a per-device convenience like the camera.
- The last camera → `UserDefaults` (`map.region`), a per-device convenience.
- **Errors and loading** (Ertem, 2026-10-19: toasts for errors, spinners where something loads): with nothing stored yet (no cities), Discover's list shows a spinner while the first sync runs and, if it failed, "Couldn't Load Spots" with Try Again and one toast (`SpotsMapModel.firstLoad`); a stored map never shows either. A shared spot that isn't stored yet shows an "Opening Spot…" toast while the sync looks for it, which turns into the not-found or unavailable failure (`isOpeningLink`). Location unavailable is a toast; location off stays an alert, for its Settings button.

## Rules

- MapKit gotchas: no annotations before the first render (`isMapReady`); never a nil `clusteringIdentifier`, and `MKAnnotationView.prepareForReuse()` puts it and `displayPriority` back to nil and `.required`, so both pin views set them again after `super` (without it a reused pin never clusters or gives way, and pins pile up; `PinReuseTests`); no live layer shadows on pins (frames are pre-baked in `PinArt`).
- Beside the card (wide windows) the Apple logo and Legal sit in the bottom trailing corner under locate, never moving with the cards on the leading edge, so the cards reach the bottom margin (Ertem, 2026-10-30, replacing "under the card"). Locate and the logo keep the cards' margins from the screen's edges (`besideMapEdges`: 23 pt, the safe area on the sensor housing's side; Ertem: locate sat too far in). MapKit lays the logo out from the leading layout margin with no public way to move it, so `layoutChrome` has `MKMapView.placeAttributionInTrailingCorner(top:trailing:bottom:)` set that margin to the room left beside them, measured from MapKit's own views (private ivars, type-checked; English's width when they can't be read; before MapKit has laid Legal out, it lays the map out once and measures again, so a wider language keeps its logo), the constants that compensate for MapKit beside it: physical `layoutMargins`, which PinLayout can't set. Otherwise the Apple logo, Legal link and locate button stay above the top sheet: up to 10 pt above the spot sheet's lowest detent, or up to half the screen on Discover's (its middle detent) (`layoutMargins`). They ride on the sheet's edge while it slides or is dragged below that, and stay put while it moves between detents above it. `SheetEdgeTracker` keeps them in step: read the edge where UIKit sets it (pan callbacks, the settle spring, the transition), never from a display link (a frame behind), and never round it.
- Pins select on the tap itself (`pinTap`); MapKit's own selection waits ~0.5 s for its double-tap gesture to fail.
- The globe is private API (`MapGlobeProjection`): names only as `#Obfuscate` literals, the runtime shape (selector encodings, struct size) checked before any read or write, and a flat map, not a crash, when it doesn't match. Re-apply it after setting `preferredConfiguration` or an appearance change.
- Flights run only on screen and not with Reduce Motion (they land at once, as region moves, Adjust Pin's locate and cluster zooms do); one asked for off screen waits for `viewDidAppear`. A touch stops a flight where it is. Region callbacks ignore flight frames and report once on landing.
- One sheet at a time on screen: Discover's panel is part of the tab and holds search itself and makes way for the spot sheet (`setCovered`, from the sheet's edge), which is presented over everything, the tab bar included. Never hide or swap the system tab bar. Never move the panel with the spot sheet's transition coordinator alone: a layout pass before the transition starts would snap it, and a swipe's alongside animations ignore the finger.
- Private API (`RegulatedCollectionView`, like `MapGlobeProjection`): names only as `#Obfuscate` literals, type encodings checked before use, installed on our own subclass only (never swizzle UIKit classes), and the public behavior when the shape doesn't match.
- Don't remove and re-add annotations as the map moves: MapKit keeps stale clusters for clustered annotations that are removed and re-added.
- `follow(_:)` acts only on selection changes. `updateProperties()` runs on every region change, and re-applying an unchanged (nil) selection would deselect the pin MapKit is selecting mid-tap.
- Simulator automation: MapKit ignores zero-length synthetic taps on annotations; drive pins with a press of about 120 ms (a Down, then an Up 120 ms later).
