# ProfileKit

**Purpose:** The Profile tab (your profile card, Collections, Privacy (Blocked Users, Share Analytics, the Privacy Policy), Appearance, Notifications, Support, Send Feedback, and the account group: how you're signed in, Sign Out, Delete Account), Edit Profile, other people's public profiles, and the report sheet.
**Tier:** Feature
**Project deps:** `AccountKit`, `AppLinkKit`, `PeopleKit`, `PhotoUploadKit` (the prepared avatar's bytes), `ImageKit`, `TipJarKit`, `NotificationsKit`, `AnalyticsKit`, `ComponentKit`, `SQLiteKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `IdentifiedCollections` (lists changed by id), PinLayout, `SPConfetti`, `SQLiteData`; tests: SnapshotTesting, `SpotModelKit`)
**Public Factory keys:** none
**Entry points:**
- `ProfileViewController` — inset-grouped list over `ProfileModel`: the profile card (`ProfileHeaderCell`, in a plain compositional section with the list's insets: an inset-grouped list clips a cell to its card's corners, which rounded the outer stat tiles more), then the rows in pairs (see Rules). Pull to refresh.
- `AppearanceViewController` + `ColorScheme` — Appearance (Ertem, 2026-10-14): "Color Scheme" and a line under it, then three tiles (System, Light, Dark; glyph over title, the chosen one outlined in `.label`, the others dim), stacked at accessibility sizes. A pick crossfades every window of the scene to it (`ColorScheme.apply(to:)`: the scene's `traitOverrides`, so the toasts' window follows too; System removes the override); `SceneDelegate` applies the stored one to the scene before it makes the window. Per device (`UserDefaults`), like the map's camera. Then App Icon: the Wheel family (`AppIcon`: Simple, Clear, Black, Swirl, Conical) as rows (`AppIconRow`: the icon, its name and a line, a check on the current one); a tap calls `setAlternateIconName` and iOS shows its own alert; a failure puts the check back. The previews are `ictool` renders of the app's Icon Composer files (`mise run ios:app-icons`), light and dark, in `Resources/Assets.xcassets`.
- `PrivacyViewController` — Privacy (Ertem, 2026-10-15: privacy things together, not a bare toggle in the list): Blocked Users (gray `person.fill.xmark`), Share Analytics (a `SwitchCell` over AnalyticsKit's `isEnabled()` / `setEnabled(_:)`, on by default; off stops the app's analytics and the server's, `DNT: 1`) with a one-line footer on why to share (Ertem, 2026-10-19: the area from the IP address is fine, so no "location never included"), and the Privacy Policy (blue `text.document.fill`, an ↗ accessory, `SFSafariViewController`).
- `EditProfileViewController` — over `EditProfileModel`: photo (the same sources as New Spot's Add Photos through ComponentKit's `PhotoPicker`, prepared like a spot photo, or Remove), name, username (kept in the case typed; 5 to 24 letters A–Z, digits or underscores, checked as you type; the server's "isn't available", for a taken name or one it doesn't allow, shows inline too; no content type, so the Passwords bar doesn't offer logins for a public handle), bio (160), the six handles as rows (glyph, network, "@" prefix; right to left the row mirrors while the "@" and what's typed stay left to right, `FieldRowView`'s `handle:`, as the username's does). Cancel/Done (VoiceOver's Escape is Cancel); changes ask before discarding. At accessibility text sizes a handle row's network name sits above its field (`FieldRowView`). While saving (the photo uploads, then the profile), Done is a spinner and Cancel and the fields are off; a failure is a toast (an unavailable username stays inline).
- `PublicProfileViewController` — someone's profile; More holds Report and Block/Unblock (blocking asks first; a spinner stands in for More while it's sent, a failure is a toast). Before a profile has ever loaded, a spinner stands where More appears; a failed first load says why with Try Again, which spins in place while it retries. An unavailable profile says so plainly.
- `BlockedUsersViewController` (pushed from Privacy; a row's Unblock spins while it's sent, a failure is a toast), `ReportViewController` (`inNavigation(target:)`; an inset-grouped list as Settings lays out a choice, Ertem 2026-10-19: the reasons in one card with a checkmark on the chosen one, then the note in ComponentKit's `TextViewListCell` with who sees reports under it; Send is a ✓ (its word for VoiceOver) and a spinner while sending, a failure is a toast and the sheet stays, the thanks a success toast).
- `NotificationSettingsViewController` + `NotificationSettingsModel` — Notifications: before iOS has asked, Turn On Notifications (iOS's prompt); after a no, Open Settings (the app's notification settings); then a switch per kind (`SwitchCell`, read by VoiceOver as one switch), the review queue only for editors and admins. The kinds show from the first frame and never leave: on the choices last loaded for this account (NotificationsKit's `cachedTopics()`), or before any, off and disabled until they load (Ertem, 2026-10-19: a spinner per row looked odd); switches are disabled while iOS has notifications off (the row above says why) and for a kind the server doesn't have yet; a switch changes at once and flips back with a failure toast if the change didn't save (a late answer never undoes a newer switch). Coming back to the app re-reads the permission.
- `SupportSheet` + `TipChoiceView` — Support OpenSkateMap (the Profile row above the account group, which spins while the tips load) as an option sheet: the title and what tips pay for on the leading edge, one card of radio rows (the tip, its price; the middle one chosen), and one white capsule, "Leave a $4.99 Tip". Confirm spins while the App Store answers; a failure is a toast and the sheet stays. Cancelling the App Store sheet keeps it open; Ask to Buy closes it with a toast that it waits for approval; paying closes it with a success haptic and confetti (SPConfetti at 80 a second, every shape, falling from the top for 2.5 s: Ertem found 24 too little and 200 too much; none with Reduce Motion), and the Support footer thanks you for the visit. No hero, app glyph or per-tier icons (Ertem, 2026-10-12); our own sheet, not a RevenueCat paywall; never the word "Donate" (App Review 3.2.2(iv)).
- `FeedbackMail` — Send Feedback (the row after Support): Mail to `feedback@openskatemap.app` (Cloudflare Email Routing forwards it to Ertem) with room to write, then a block with the version, system and model, UTC time, language and region, time zone and the account id, and this run's log attached as `OpenSkateMapLog.txt` (`OSLogStore`, the app's subsystem, `.notice` and above, private values redacted as the system logs them; read from the process's launch, not the device's boot, which on a phone up for days was slow; never empty, since Mail drops an empty attachment). The row spins while it's prepared. Without a Mail account it opens a `mailto:` link (no log), and failing that offers the address to copy. Mail failing to send is a toast.
- `ProfileScreens` — `publicProfile(userID:)` and `reportSpot(id:)`: what the app binds SpotDetailKit's seams to.
- `ProfileHeaderView` — the card both profiles open with (Ertem, 2026-10-14): the 80 pt avatar on the leading side; the name, @username with the role badge after it and bio beside it (centred against the avatar when shorter; no join date, Ertem: not needed); the three stats as rounded tiles below (rounded numerals over the caption, one VoiceOver phrase each, `tileColor` a step up from the background); then, on public profiles, the handle chips. At accessibility text sizes the text goes under the avatar and the tiles stack. Laid out from the leading edge (`start`), so it mirrors right to left.
- `HandleChipsView`, `AvatarView`, `FieldRowView` — the pieces those screens share. `AvatarView` shows the photo through ImageKit's `PhotoLoader` (`PhotoSize.avatar`, its ThumbHash first), or initials.

- **Collections:** a Collections row on Profile pushes `collectionsListScreen`; a public profile's More menu has Collections, which pushes `userCollectionsScreen(userID)`. Both are promised seams the app binds to CollectionDetailKit.

**Ownership map:** the account shown → `AuthSession.phase`; profiles and the blocked list → the people cache in SQLite (PeopleKit's `PeopleStore`), observed with `@Fetch` (`ProfileRequest`, `BlockedUsersRequest`) and refreshed through `ProfileSession`; the draft → `EditProfileModel`; deletion in flight → `ProfileModel.isDeletingAccount`.

## Rules

- **Cache-first, no jumps (Ertem, 2026-10-11).** Every screen opens on the cached copy and refreshes in the background, changing in place. Every section is there from the first frame: before a profile has ever loaded on this device, the card shows what's known (your name and role from the account) and a dash for the rest, in the same places (`ProfileHeaderView.showPlaceholder`). No loading rows that later disappear. A failed refresh keeps what's shown; only with nothing cached does the Profile card's footer say it couldn't load. A pull to refresh that fails says so in a toast; the screen's own refreshes never do. Blocked Users shows its loading state only before the list has ever loaded.
- Deleting asks for confirmation, says what happens to contributions, and on success the session ends with the `.accountDeleted` notice on sign-in (HIG: account deletion). A failure is a toast, except a 401: that already ended the session and sign-in says so.
- **Errors and progress (Ertem, 2026-10-19).** A failed action is a failure toast (`ToastView.showFailure`), never an alert; alerts are for confirmations and choices. What's being sent shows a spinner where it was started (a bar button turns into `UIBarButtonItem.spinner()`, a button sets `showsActivityIndicator`, a row its accessory). An app icon change that fails puts the check back and says so.
- The email is shown only here, to its owner, under "Using Sign in with Apple". A tap on that row opens a popover (ComponentKit's `ActionPopoverController`) with just Copy Email and Copy User ID (Ertem, 2026-10-19: no icons, not the values). A long press on a profile's @username offers the system's Copy, as on selected text (`CopyableLabel`; a VoiceOver action too, off while it's a placeholder). Copying says nothing, like Copy Link and Copy Address.
- Emptying a field in Edit Profile clears it on the server (`ProfileUpdate.clear`); nothing is sent for fields that didn't change.
- Profile tab order: the profile card (no handle chips; your links are edited in Edit Profile only, Ertem 2026-10-14: no Social Media screen) → Collections and Privacy (blue hand) → Appearance (teal) and Notifications (red bell, Ertem asked for notification settings, 2026-10-13) → Support OpenSkateMap (pink heart) and Send Feedback (indigo envelope) → **Account**: Using Sign in with Apple, Sign Out and Delete Account together (Ertem, 2026-10-10). Rows carry no footers (Ertem, 2026-10-14: too much text); the only ones say the profile couldn't load, thank you after a tip, and say what deleting the account means. A footer that must exist stays within two lines on an iPhone 17 Pro (Ertem, 2026-10-15: four lines is too much, one cheap line isn't better). No "What's in OpenSkateMap" row (Ertem dropped it). Edit Profile is the pencil in the navigation bar, enabled once the profile has loaded; the title is `.inline`. The list ends with `LegalFooterView`, the layout's boundary item (not a section's): Privacy Policy · Terms of Use as links (`LegalPage`, AppLinkKit, the same pages sign-in links; they open in `SFSafariViewController`) and the version, small and centered (App Review 5.1.1(i): the privacy policy reachable in the app).
- Settings rows lead with an app-icon tile (`UIImage.tile` / `symbolTile`, ComponentKit): Apple black, Collections orange, Privacy blue (`hand.raised.fill`), Appearance teal (`circle.lefthalf.filled`), Share Analytics purple (`chart.bar.fill`, in Privacy), Sign Out gray, Delete Account red (red text). Ertem: icons in settings must look like app icons, never bare glyphs or logos.
- Pull to refresh is ComponentKit's `PullToRefreshControl` (at least 0.5 s, ends after the finger lifts); a pull while a load runs waits for that one.
- Networks in settings rows (the link rows, Edit Profile) are app-icon tiles (`HandleNetwork.tile(side:traitCollection:)`): the brand's glyph in white on its color (Instagram's gradient, YouTube red, Telegram blue, X black), TikTok's own three-color note on black, the website's globe on gray. The glyphs are Simple Icons' monochrome SVGs (CC0; the marks stay their owners' trademarks), cropped to their bounds and converted to template vector PDFs (`Resources/Assets.xcassets/<network>-glyph`); Telegram's is only the plane from its circle mark.
- The public profile's chips keep the networks' own full-color logos (`HandleNetwork.icon(side:traitCollection:)`): svgl.app SVGs as vector PDFs (TikTok and X have dark variants), never SF Symbol stand-ins.
- Other features are reached through promised Factory keys, never imported.

## Tests

`ProfileScreensSnapshotTests` snapshots the Profile tab and a public profile at 1x, cold (placeholders), cached (the first frame, taken before the refresh can run) and refreshed in place, and the cached tab right to left, over an in-memory people cache. `ProfileModelTests` checks that a failed public profile keeps its message while a retry runs. `SettingsTileTests` snapshots the settings rows' tiles (the six networks, then the account group) in light and dark at 2x. Record with `TEST_RUNNER_SNAPSHOT_TESTING_RECORD=all` on the iOS 27 simulator after an intended change and look at both images before committing them.
