# ContributeKit

**Purpose:** The Contribute tab (Near You, an add-a-spot card, ways to help, and "Your Spots" with what's on its way and what curators decided), the New Spot sheet, and the curators' review queue.
**Tier:** Feature
**Project deps:** `AccountKit`, `AppLinkKit`, `ContributionsKit`, `PhotoUploadKit`, `CollectionsKit`, `ReviewKit`, `SpotModelKit`, `ImageKit`, `LocationKit`, `ComponentKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `IdentifiedCollections` (lists changed by id), `PinLayout`, `SQLiteData`)
**Public Factory keys:** `contributeTabOpener` — promised: shows the Contribute tab (the app binds it; it owns the tabs and closes a spot sheet covering them). `spotEditOpener` — promised: opens a spot on the map by id with its Suggest an Edit over it (the app binds it: closes what's presented, selects Discover, `openSpot(id:suggestingEdit:)`).
**Entry points:**
- `ContributeViewController` — the tab (`Mode.tab`), and Your Spots in full behind its See All (`Mode.yourSpots`: the same rows, no other sections, no section header). What yours add up to (`ContributeModel.Summary`: added · on the map · waiting) is the navigation bar's subtitle, nothing before the first spot. Sections in order:
  - "Near You" (`NearYouRequest`, `NearYouCell`): up to 20 public, not demolished spots from the local index within about 55 km, those without a photo first, then nearest, leaving out spots the person already has an edit on its way or waiting for (an unsent draft that edits it, or a pending submission with a spot id, which only an edit has) until it's decided. Around the person's last fix when location is already allowed (LocationKit's `lastLocation()`, read off the main actor, never asked for here; then each card says how far), else the Map tab's last camera (no distance), else no section (`NearYou.hasOrigin`: with nowhere to look there's no "around here" to speak of); then, when allowed and at most every 5 minutes, a fresh fix (`currentLocation()`, no prompt once allowed) moves it when it's over 500 m from the one shown, since the last fix may be from another city. The origin is rounded to about 100 m and read again when the tab appears; the query loads with `.backgroundRead`. The header's trailing text is the nearest spot's city. A card: the cover through ImageKit (after its ThumbHash), or the kind's glyph on its tint; over it one true line about what the spot is missing (`NearYou.Ask`, computed by the request, the most useful first: no cover → "No one's shot this one yet", no obstacles → "No obstacles listed yet", nothing when it has both; a photo count from the server would add "Only one photo so far" there; never benefit copy), wrapping to two lines; the name; distance · first obstacle (or kind); Add Photos, a primary capsule on spots without a photo and a fill on the rest. Cards are 168 pt wide, scaled with the text size, in a row that scrolls sideways from the 16 pt margins, all as tall as the tallest, measured (a sideways row keeps estimated heights). A tap opens the spot on the map; VoiceOver reads a card as one element (the name, then distance and what's there, then the ask) with Add Photos as an action. Somewhere to look with no spots around it shows the section with one quiet line, "No spots around here yet", laid out as a list, and no button: the Add a Spot card right below does the asking.
  - Add Photos (Ertem, 2026-11-27: no flow of its own) opens the spot on the map and its Suggest an Edit over the sheet (Edit Spot for an admin) through `spotEditOpener`, the same sheet as the spot's More → Suggest an Edit, where photos are added, credited and sent.
  - `ContributeHeroView` (in `ContributeHeroCell`): the tab's only "Add a Spot", one `UIControl` so the whole card is the tap target (Ertem, 2026-11-27: the old dark card with a white capsule competed with Near You's cards). A slab in the website's accent (#F5520A) under static film grain, black type (white on that orange falls under 4.5:1 for small text): the headline in compressed heavy caps, the message under it, and a see-through "+ Add a Spot" pill (ink at 10% with a 1 pt rim at 30%) beside the text, or under it when the message would run past about two lines, the pill is too wide, or at accessibility sizes. Pressing scales it to 0.97 over 0.3 s on a (0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) curve; with Reduce Motion it dims instead. Same 16 pt margins as the headers; before any spot it says what adding means, afterwards "Got another spot?" with what can be added. VoiceOver reads it as one "Add a Spot" button with the headline and message as its value.
  - "Ways to Help": the Review Queue for curators, with how many wait (`ContributeModel.reviewWaiting`: the last known count, kept through reloads, from `ReviewSession.isQueueKnown`), as a row with a `UIImage.symbolTile` icon.
  - Spacing: sections are `sectionSpacing` apart (the layout's `interSectionSpacing`) and every header has the same even margins (`sectionHeaderMargins`), so the gaps match whichever sections show and a header's trailing accessory (Near You's city, Your Spots' See All) centres on its title.
  - "Your Spots": the latest three on the tab, with See All in the header when there are more. Rows (`YourSpotCell`): the cover photo (ImageKit, after its ThumbHash; an unsent entry shows only the ThumbHash) with a status badge over it (Sending, with a small spinner while it's on its way, Waiting for network, Failed + reason (ContributionsKit's `OutboxProblem.message`, never the server's words) + Retry, Pending, Approved, Rejected + reason, Private + "Added to <collection>": never reviewed, so never "Approved"; Removed + the reason's `explanation` when an admin took the spot down, which the summary no longer counts as added), the title, the city (the kind outside our cities) and date. A pending row says who checks it ("With the curators. Every spot gets a look.", an edit "With the spot's owner or a curator for a look"). A rejected row keeps the reason and adds a way back phrased from it (`YourSpot.WayBack`, derived in `YourSpotsRequest`: Already on the map → "Add your photos to the one that's there", whose Add Photos opens the public spot within 150 m of it in the local index, and no button when there's none; an edit rejected as a duplicate → "Got a shot this spot doesn't have yet?"; not a spot, wrong pin, private property, photo rights, and any other reason → "Got another angle?", each its own line) with Try Again: an edit's opens its spot's Suggest an Edit (`spotEditOpener`), a new spot's opens New Spot, and private property always opens New Spot, since editing the same place doesn't fix it. The way back is a medium-weight line under the reason, the button under it (the one Retry uses), wrapping at any text size. A published row (approved, or private, and not removed) opens its spot on the map (`appLinkOpener`). VoiceOver reads a row as one element (title, then status, details, reason, way back), with Retry, Try Again or Add Photos as an action. At accessibility text sizes the status badge moves from the thumbnail to under the details, which wrap. Swipe to discard an unsent entry; pull to refresh. Empty, the section says what will show up there. Failures a person caused are failure toasts (`ToastView.showFailure`): a pull whose history didn't come back (ComponentKit's `PullToRefreshControl`: the spinner stays at least 0.5 s and ends when the finger lifts), a Retry that couldn't be written, a discard of an entry that started uploading meanwhile ("It's already on its way"). The outbox's own passes never toast: their failures stay on the rows.

The pick's credit step comes once its videos are through, and covers a clip's photo like any other. With Reduce Motion a dragged card doesn't tilt, snaps back without a bounce, and fades once decided instead of flying off. - **New Spot → Visibility:** the Public Map (reviewed, the default) or one of the account's private collections (`NewSpotModel.privateCollections`, `@FetchAll`), plus New Private Collection. **Ownership map:**
- The spot being created → `NewSpotModel` (its `NewSpotForm`), owned by the sheet; discarded on close (confirmed when anything was entered). Option sheets edit copies and apply them on Confirm.
- Unsent and sent spots → SQLite (ContributionsKit's outbox), read through `YourSpotsRequest`.

## Rules

- Haptics confirm outcomes only: success when a spot, edit or video is in the outbox; the review decisions above; a selection tick when an obstacle chip toggles.
- HIG: one sheet at a time from the form; option sheets stack on it and close before the next opens. Location is asked for only from "Use My Location" or the locate button; the photo picker needs no library permission.
- Someone else's photo needs a name and the permission switch before its Confirm (and the ✓) enables (product spec, photo rights).
- Search suggestions and a dropped pin start around the Map tab's last camera (`map.region` in `UserDefaults`, written by SpotsMapKit), read only.
- The review stack's visuals are plain on purpose.
- Take Photo needs `NSCameraUsageDescription` (the app target); it's offered only when `UIImagePickerController.isSourceTypeAvailable(.camera)`.
- New scroll views chain `.softEdges()` (lint `soft_scroll_edges`).
- The review queue is owned by `ReviewSession` (cache-first through ReviewKit's `ReviewQueueStore`); this module never stores cards. An edit's card shows "Suggested edit" with each change as before → after (`ReviewCardView.changes`; photos as what it adds, what it removes, or "Reorders the photos"), the photos as the edit leaves them (credits from ReviewKit's `Photo.attribution`: the spot's own "One of the spot's photos" when the uploader took them), and no photo area when it doesn't change them; Edit Before Approving changes only the suggested fields. The editor approves the card it was opened for (by id), not whatever is on top. Notice copy is shared (`ReviewSession.Notice.toast`): the video list shows notices too, and has the queue's loading, failure and settling states, and the Review Queue row counts cards and videos. A reject reason's label lives in one place (`RejectReason+Title`) for both the curator and the submitter.

## Tests
