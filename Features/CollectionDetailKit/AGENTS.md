# CollectionDetailKit

**Purpose:** The collection screens: the list of collections, one collection (its spots on a map and as a list), Save to Collection, and the owner's Share sheet.
**Tier:** Feature
**Project deps:** `AppLinkKit`, `SpotModelKit`, `CollectionsKit`, `PhotoUploadKit`, `SpotsKit`, `ImageKit`, `ComponentKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `IdentifiedCollections` (lists changed by id), `PinLayout`, `SQLiteData`; tests: `SQLiteKit`, `AccountKit`, `CustomDump`, `SnapshotTesting`)
**Public Factory keys:** none (a spot and Show on Map open through AppLinkKit's `appLinkOpener`)
**Entry points:**
- `CollectionScreens` — what the app binds to other features' seams:
  - `list()`: the account's collections: "Invitations" first (each opens the collection's Accept / Decline page; Profile is where a missed invitation notification can be found again), "Yours" (owner) then "Shared with You" (admin, editor or viewer, "Shared by <owner>"; the same collections Discover's cards show), with + (ProfileKit's `collectionsListScreen`).
  - `publicCollections(of:)`: someone's public ones (ProfileKit's `userCollectionsScreen`).
  - `collection(id:)`: one collection with Close (✕) on the leading side, presented for a link (`AppLink.collection`).
  - `picker(spotID:)`: Save to Collection (SpotDetailKit's `collectionPickerScreen`).
  - `newCollection()`: the name prompt (`NewCollectionAlert`) for Discover's + (SpotsMapKit's `newCollectionScreen`); once made, the collection opens through `appLinkOpener` as a link does.
- `CollectionViewController` — the name as the large title, "Yours · Private, shared with 2" under it, a still map of the spots (`CollectionMapView`: a MapKit snapshot with a dot per spot in its kind's colour), then the spots. Below the hero the page's top corners round over it (42 pt, concentric with the action capsules, Show on Map and Share, which stack at full width at accessibility text sizes) and flatten in step with the scroll, square exactly as the page reaches the top of the screen (Ertem, 2026-10-19); the hero leaves that shape unpainted (`CollectionHeroView.pageOverlap`), so nothing covers the rows. The hero is in the list's background view: right to left, the list flips its own layer and unflips only the views it manages, so a hero added to the list itself drew its photo and title mirrored (Arabic, 2026-10-21; `CollectionSnapshotTests` covers it right to left). A spot opens on the Discover map. With none: "No Spots Yet", and for those who can add, how (a spot's More → Save to Collection).
  - Owner: Share (the access sheet), Rename, Delete, and swipe to remove a spot.
  - Someone it's shared with: Leave.
  - Opened by a link the device doesn't have: fetched online (`CollectionsSession.page(of:)`), with a loading, a gone and a failed state. Its spots come a page at a time (200): the next is asked for 30 rows before the end (`CollectionModel.loadMoreSpots()`, one at a time, a spinner row under the list while it loads; a failed one is asked for again on the next scroll and toasts once per streak). A collection the account is in is in SQLite whole.
  - One that was on the device and vanishes while open (deleted, or the account was taken off) says "No Longer Available" (`Phase.gone`), never an endless spinner.
  - In flight (`CollectionModel.action`, one at a time): Accept, Decline and Ask to Join spin in their capsule with both disabled; Delete and Leave put a spinner in place of •••; a spot being removed spins on its row (`removing`).
- `CollectionAccessViewController` — the owner's Share sheet, as sharing a page works:
  - Private (you and the people you add) or Public (anyone signed in with the link; your profile lists it). Either change asks first (`CollectionAccessModel.confirmation(for:)`, the only way to `confirm(_:)` one, Ertem 2026-10-19): going public says who will see it, with the private spots inside when there are some; going private says anyone who only has the link loses it and it leaves your profile, while the people added keep it.
  - People: Add People (the whole row is the button: a popover, ComponentKit's `ActionPopoverController`, a popover on iPhone too, picks the role first, Ertem 2026-10-19; then an alert takes the @username or the email they signed in with: Invite / Cancel, HIG's at most three buttons, Invite enabled once what's typed is one), members and pending invites, each removable by swipe; a member's role menu also ends with Remove from Collection.
  - Changes in flight are per row (`CollectionAccessModel.changes`): the row spins and takes no second one, others stay usable. Answers are numbered: one older than an answer already shown is dropped and the list is read again once all are in. A write that went through is never reported as failed because the read after it failed: the shown list takes the write's effect instead.
  - Copy Link and Share Link (`openskatemap.app/collections/<id>`), only once the link opens it for someone (`showsLinks`): public, or private with someone in it or invited (Ertem, 2026-10-19). The list animates as that changes.
- `CollectionPickerViewController` — the account's own collections with a check where the spot is, and New Collection (its row spins while the collection is made). The server answers before the check moves, a spinner on the row meanwhile; a private spot someone else added can't be saved elsewhere, which the failure toast says. The sheet is as tall as its rows (a custom detent from the laid-out list, at most 60% of the screen, re-resolved when rows change), with large to grow into.
- `CollectionEditViewController` — Edit Collection: the photo (Choose Photo, Remove Photo), the name (not for Favorites) and the description. No colour or pattern (Ertem, 2026-10-19: unused); the colour of a collection without a picture is the server's. Saving puts a spinner in place of Done and locks Cancel (and VoiceOver's Escape), swipe-to-close and the fields; Choose Photo spins while the photo is prepared, and a photo that can't be used toasts.
- `NewCollectionAlert` — the name prompt the list's +, the picker and Discover's + share; Create is enabled once there's a name. The list's + becomes a spinner while the collection is made.
- Your collections with nothing stored wait for the first pull (`.loading()`) and show the failed state if it fails (`CollectionsSession.isSyncing` / `lastOutcome`); pull to refresh toasts a failure only over a list that has rows.

**Ownership map:** collections and their spots → SQLite, written by `CollectionsSync` (CollectionsKit) and read here with `@Fetch` (`LocalCollectionRequest`, `CollectionsListRequest`, `SavedInRequest`); who a collection is shared with → the server, live in the Share sheet.

## Rules

- **Failures are toasts** (Ertem, 2026-10-19): `CollectionFailure.show(_:_:notFound:)`, the "Couldn't …" title over why (`message(for:)`, or what a 404 means there). Alerts are only for confirmations (Delete, Leave, Make Public/Private) and text input (New Collection, Add People). Background pulls never toast.
- Screens open from cache: lists and the collection read SQLite; only a link to a collection the device doesn't have, someone's public list and the Share sheet are online.
- No live map in a list: `CollectionMapView` is a snapshot, like the spot sheet's map card (and a live `MKMapView` crashes hostless snapshot renders).
- Snapshot tests (`CollectionSnapshotTests`) seed an in-memory database and a session whose pulls fail, so the cache stays as seeded. Sheets are snapshotted without their navigation controller (with a Done button, a hostless render draws nothing).
