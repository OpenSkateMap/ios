# SpotDetailKit

**Purpose:** The spot sheet over the map and its full page, photo first: cover, title, what the spot is, actions, location, obstacles, about, who added it, how skaters rate it, and videos.
**Tier:** Feature
**Project deps:** `SpotsKit`, `ContributionsKit`, `CollectionsKit`, `SpotModelKit`, `ImageKit`, `LocationKit`, `AnalyticsKit`, `ComponentKit`, `AppLinkKit`, `AccountKit` (the account's role), `SQLiteKit` (packages: `FactoryKit`, `PinLayout`, `SQLiteData`; tests: `Nuke`, SnapshotTesting)
**Public Factory keys:** `userProfileScreen`, `spotReportScreen` (promised `ParameterFactory`s: bound by the app to ProfileKit's screens)
**Entry points:**
- `SpotSheetViewController(selection:)` — beside the map in a wide window (ComponentKit's `placeBesideMap()`, as are the sheets it opens): on the leading edge on iOS 27, as wide as iOS allows an edge-attached sheet (about 500 pt in landscape, whatever width is asked); iOS 26 keeps the system's placement. Two detents: the summary (`smallDetentHeight`, 420 pt: cover, title, meta line, action tiles) and large (the whole page). Undimmed up to the summary so the map stays usable. In compact height iOS 27 floats the sheet 8 pt above the bottom yet keeps it full height, past the top edge, so there the summary is the screen less 8 pt at each end, the large detent drops out, and the page's summary is as tall as the sheet (Ertem, 2026-10-19).
- `SpotPageView` — the page, laid out from `pageRows`, an ordered list of rows with the space before each. A new section is one `SpotPageSectionView` (optionally with a header accessory) and one entry there.
- `SpotSheetModel` — `@Fetch(SpotPageRequest)` for the open spot (index row, detail, photos, city in one read), its address (looked up again whenever the spot moves, the model watching its own page) and `isGone` (the spot left the index). One spot at a time: `show(_:)` cancels what loads for the last.

- **Collections:** More → Save to Collection opens `collectionPickerScreen` (a promised seam, bound by the app to CollectionDetailKit's picker). A private spot (`SpotIndex.isPrivate`) shows no Videos section, no Add Video, no Suggest an Edit and no Rating: it isn't curated, and the server refuses its videos, edits and votes.

**Ownership map:** the open spot → `SpotSelection` (SpotsKit); the page → SQLite, filled by `DetailFetcher`; photos → the shared Nuke pipeline through `SpotPhotoLoader`; the clip player → `ClipPlayback`, owned by the cover card and lent to the viewer.
- **Details not stored yet:** the row under the page reads "Loading details…" with a spinner while the open spot's fetch runs (`SpotsSession.selectedDetails`), and "Couldn't load the details. Try Again" with one failure toast per attempt when it failed; a spot with stored details never shows it.

## Rules

- **Photo first.** The cover card is the top element on both detents. It pages through the spot's photos with a swipe (`CoverCardView`): the cover first, straight from the index row, then the rest once the details load. Each photo is its own rounded card in a slot, paged with a 12 pt gap (the system photo viewer's page gap at a card's scale), and no band over it (Ertem, 2026-10-11: the condition isn't shown there; the line under the title names it only when it's a warning, not "Skateable"). The system `UIPageControl` sits at the photo's bottom (system dots, a platter only while touched, taps animate and scrubbing jumps; hidden for one photo), and VoiceOver pages with swipe up and down, hearing who took each photo. There's no separate Photos section (Ertem, 2026-10-11): the cover is the gallery, and credits live on the photos. A spot without a photo gets its kind's colour as a soft gradient with a small glyph, never a giant glyph or an empty card; with photos, their ThumbHashes stand in, and a photo out in the viewer leaves its neutral slot, as a cell in a photo grid does. The summary sizes the cover to fill what the title, meta line and tiles leave, so it always ends right under the tiles. No icon tile next to the title. The star (Favorites) sits in the photo's bottom trailing corner, 10 pt from its bottom and trailing edges (Ertem, 2026-10-19; bottom-right left to right). Right to left the photos page from the right, the next one coming from the left, as the system photo viewer does (Ertem, 2026-10-22; ComponentKit's `layoutRow`, and the viewer's `UIPageViewController` mirrors itself); the photos never flip.
- **Clips.** A photo with a clip (`SpotPhoto.clip`, a short silent MP4 straight from the photos domain, `PhotoURLBuilder.clipURL(for:)`) plays it muted and looped in its card, the photo being its poster: the clip sits inside the photo, transparent until its first frame is drawn (ComponentKit's `ClipLayerView`), so the poster shows until then, never a black frame. Only the settled page plays (paging away, the clip keeps playing as it slides out and stops once the next page lands), and only while the card is on screen (`CoverCardView.isOnScreen`: the sheet is up, no sheet of ours hides it, and the card isn't scrolled away; a sheet above reports its dismissal through `WindowExitProbe`). The viewer plays the page on screen and gets the same player (`ClipPlayback`, the sheet's one player: the view that asks last gets it, the same clip asked for again keeps playing where it was, and it goes once no view shows it), so opening and closing never start a clip over. With Auto-Play Video Previews off or in Low Power Mode (ComponentKit's `ClipAutoplay`, the rule the review cards follow too), the card shows the poster with a small glass play glyph and the clip plays only in the viewer, opened on purpose.
- **Photo viewer** (`PhotoViewerController`), measured against the system photo viewer (iOS 27 `PUOneUpSettings`/`PUTilingViewSettings` defaults) and checked side by side on the simulator: 40 pt between pages (the system's on iPhone), the current page and its neighbours kept (a page further away is made again from the image cache, so a spot's ten full-screen photos aren't all held at once); the system background behind glass controls (close, "2 of 5", "Photo by …" from `SpotPhoto.credit`), and a tap or zooming in hides them, the status bar and turns the background black (`UINavigationController.hideShowBarDuration`). Double-tap zooms a letterboxed photo to fill the screen, others to 2.5× (the system's `doubleTapZoomFactor`), at the tapped point; a photo left behind fits again (`PhotoPageViewController`, the card's photo standing in until the full-screen variant loads). Not UIKit's zoom transition (it moves the whole screen as a card, which drew a white card and its edge around the photo): `PhotoViewerTransition` flies a copy of the photo between the card and the screen, from the card's crop and corners to the whole photo, square, while the background (its own view, faded by alpha) and controls fade; opening is the system's 0.5 s spring with a slight overshoot, closing 0.3 s with no bounce; the card's controls (the star, `cardControls`) dissolve away with the opening flight and come back as the photo lands (Ertem, 2026-10-20; `isHidden` inside the animation, since glass doesn't draw at partial alpha); the viewer is presented over full screen so the sheet shows through. A swipe down is the system viewer's (`PXSwipeDownSettings`/`PXSwipeDownTracker`, iOS 27): down follows the finger, up and sideways give way logarithmically over 100 pt, the photo scales `0.5 + 0.5·cos(atan(dy/200))` around the finger, the background fades over 400 pt, the card's photo leaves its slot while it's out; letting go closes it unless the finger last moved up (5 pt filter), from wherever the photo is and at the finger's speed, otherwise it springs back (0.3 s, no bounce). Only a downward swipe on a photo that isn't zoomed in starts it; paging and panning wait for it to be ruled out. With Reduce Motion it crossfades.
- **Actions.** Directions is the primary tile and opens a menu (`DirectionsApp`): Apple Maps (`MKMapItem.openInMaps`, directions mode), Google Maps and Yandex Maps through their universal links (their app when installed, the website otherwise; no `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`). Share sends the title and the spot's link (`AppLink`, `openskatemap.app/spots/<id>`), which opens the spot in the app; `SpotLinkItem` builds its preview (title and cover photo) on the device, since the link's web page names no spot. Copy Address is the last item of Directions' menu (once the address is known) and a long press on the address or the map card (`UIContextMenuInteraction`) (Ertem, 2026-10-11). More holds Save to Collection, Suggest an Edit (once the details are in; the app's `suggestEditScreen` with a `SpotEditTarget` built from the page and the address; it says Edit on your own spot and for admins, whose edits apply at once, Ertem 2026-10-19) and Report Spot (`spotReportScreen`), then for admins Delete Spot (Ertem, 2026-10-20), destructive and last: it opens `SpotDeleteViewController` (Report's shape, Ertem 2026-10-20: the reasons as a list, `SpotRemovalReason`, presets only, a footer saying the submitter's Your Spots shows the reason's `explanation`; ✕, and a ✓ once a reason is picked), whose `SpotDeleteModel` calls `SpotsSession.takeDown(_:reason:)`; the spot sheet closes itself as the spot leaves the index, taking the screen with it (the map dismisses from its own side), and a success toast offers Undo for 5 s (`SpotsSession.restore(_:)`, then a collections pull for a private spot); a refusal is a failure toast, the screen staying open; no Add Video (the Videos header's button) and no Open in Maps, which is Directions' job. Report Condition isn't built yet; don't add it disabled. No sticky Directions capsule at the bottom. At accessibility text sizes the three tiles become full-width rows, glyph leading (`ActionTilesView`; HIG Typography: fewer columns as text grows). The line under the title is kind · city · a warning condition; no obstacles (Ertem, 2026-10-14: they have their own section). Directions and a completed Share are analytics events (AnalyticsKit).
- **Title bar** (`SpotTitleBarView`, v1.0 scope 2026-10-20): once the page's title scrolls under it, a bar under the grabber fades in (0.2 s) with the spot's name and small glass Directions and More (the tiles' own menus), over the scroll view's soft top edge, which shows only while the bar does; the page's own title steps aside meanwhile, so the two never overlap. No ✕: the sheet stays swipe-only.
- **Location** is a still map snapshot centred on the spot (muted, it carries the Apple Maps logo), with no pin (Ertem, 2026-10-11), not a live map: it scrolls with the page. The snapshot is kept as the variant for the traits it was taken with (`resolved(with:)`), and taken again when the size or appearance changes. Tapping it asks which maps app to get directions in (an action sheet with Directions' apps, `DirectionsApp`).
- **Rating** is its own section after About (`SpotRatingView`, moved off the title's row at Ertem's ask, 2026-10-11): two equal capsules, thumbs up and thumbs down (`hand.thumbsup`/`hand.thumbsdown`) with their counts; your vote filled in the tint with the `.fill` symbol and a bounce (none with Reduce Motion), a second tap taking it back, a selection haptic on each tap. Votes go through `SpotsSession.vote(_:on:)` only, which shows the vote on the stored detail at once and falls back to the server's totals on failure, with a failure toast. Disabled until the spot's detail is stored; hidden for private spots. There is no curators' rating (dropped 2026-10-20): the score comes from riders' votes, saves, media and activity.
- **Videos** are footage first (`SpotVideosSectionView`, reworked 2026-10-11 at Ertem's ask): full-bleed 16:9 cards with the caption and "YouTube · Added by …" (two lines at accessibility text sizes) in white over a bottom scrim and a clear-glass play button. One video spans the page; more page sideways, snapping to each card with the next one peeking in. Instagram has no thumbnail without an API key, so its card is the same dark frame with the same type (no brand gradient). Add Video is the section header's trailing button (`SectionHeaderView` accessory), not a card; an empty section says "No videos of this spot yet". A video of yours still in the outbox shows its state under the cards: sending, or why it failed (ContributionsKit's `OutboxProblem.message`) with Remove.
- **One sheet at a time.** The spot sheet is sheet level `spot`, and the sheets it opens (Save to Collection, Report Spot, Delete Spot, Add Video, Suggest an Edit, the "Added by" profile) go through `presentAbove(_:)`, which hides it while they're up and brings it back when they close (ComponentKit's sheet levels; Ertem, 2026-10-11). Share, the photo viewer and the maps-app chooser aren't sheets of ours and present as usual.
- "Added by" opens that person's profile (`userProfileScreen`) when the server named them; it's hidden when it didn't (the person was deleted, or you blocked them).
- **Grey text, fills and separators are the `sheet…` colours** (`UIColor+Sheet.swift`: `sheetSecondaryLabel`, `sheetTertiaryLabel`, `sheetSecondaryFill`, `sheetTertiaryFill`, `sheetSeparator`), never the system's own on a view: the system colours resolved by appearance, level and contrast only. The sheet's glass changes a trait of its own on every expansion and collapse while the public traits stay equal (logged on the device), and colours resolved with it drew lighter until it changed back, about 0.35 s after the sheet settled: the meta line and section headers stayed dim, the obstacle chips blinked (Ertem, 2026-10-20; measured frame by frame in dark mode). A fill goes on a view of its own, never a label's `backgroundColor`, which a label paints into its text. Button configurations (the tiles, the rating capsules) resolve their colours themselves. `withProminence(.primary)` is not the fix: it draws them as the primary label colour, white or black.
- The tint comes from the cover's ThumbHash average colour (or the kind's colour), so it shows before any image loads.
- The sheet clears the selection as a swipe-down begins (`presentationControllerWillDismiss`), so the map deselects the pin alongside; a swipe given up restores it.
- When the open spot leaves the index (removed on the server), the sheet closes itself by clearing the selection.
- **Opening fast.** Making the sheet does no I/O: the model's `@Fetch` starts empty and reads in `show(_:)`, the photo loader resolves the pipeline on its first photo (`@LazyInjected`), and the map card renders its snapshot asynchronously. Measured on the simulator (Debug), the first sheet after launch is made in ~40 ms, of which ~20 ms is CoreText loading the tiles' semibold footnote face once per process; later sheets take 2–7 ms.

## Tests

`SpotPageViewTests` snapshots the page at 1x without the network: the summary and the full page with photos (and a thumbs-up vote of yours in Rating), the summary of a spot without one, the page at an accessibility text size, the summary and page right to left (Directions on the right, the star bottom left), and the cover right to left, unclipped, with the second photo waiting on the first's left. Photos come from `Tests/SpotPhoto*.jpg` (frames of Pexels clips, `NOTICE.md`) seeded into a test pipeline's memory cache. The map card stays empty (its snapshot is asynchronous) and the glass Share and More tiles don't draw outside a window. Record with `TEST_RUNNER_SNAPSHOT_TESTING_RECORD=all` on the iOS 27 simulator after an intended design change and review every image before committing it.
